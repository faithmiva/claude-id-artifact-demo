However, internal human resource records reveal that voluntary turnover among high-performing middle managers has escalated to 22% over the same period. Furthermore, exit interviews (conducted off-site) suggest that staff completed the engagement survey positively out of fear that response anonymity had been compromised. ✓Final Key Takeaways

Executive Summary

Management thought is not an abstract catalogue of historical theories, but a disciplined lens for interrogating executive practice, organisational structures, and research design. Senior executives and DBA candidates must cultivate epistemic humility, recognising that empirical data does not speak for itself but is interpreted through specific philosophical filters. African organisational contexts must not be treated as imperfect copies of Western models; indigenous practices such as the Igbo Apprenticeship System represent rich, legitimate foundations for original management theory building.

Practitioner Action Items

  • Audit Organisational Control Systems: Critically examine existing dashboards, audit routines and KPIs to identify hidden assumptions about human motivation, authority and control.
  • Articulate Epistemic Stances in Decision-Making: Before approving major strategies, explicitly define the evidentiary assumptions guiding the proposal.
  • Integrate Critical Realism into Executive Inquiry: When investigating persistent failures, design diagnostic frameworks that uncover hidden structural mechanisms.
  • Advance Context-Sensitive Management Theory: Treat local and African organisational realities as primary sources of management knowledge generation.
