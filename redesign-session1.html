Final Key Takeaways

Executive Summary

Philosophy as the Foundation of Executive Practice

Management thought is a disciplined lens for interrogating executive practice, organisational structures, and research design. Senior executives and DBA candidates must cultivate epistemic humility, recognising that empirical data is interpreted through specific philosophical filters. African organisational contexts must not be treated as imperfect copies of Western models.

Practitioner Action Items

Strategic Directives for Scholar-Practitioners

Audit Organisational Control Systems
Critically examine dashboards, audit routines and KPIs to identify hidden assumptions about human motivation, authority and control.
Articulate Epistemic Stances in Decision-Making
Before approving major strategies, explicitly define the evidentiary assumptions guiding the proposal.
Integrate Critical Realism into Executive Inquiry
When investigating persistent failures, design diagnostic frameworks that uncover hidden structural mechanisms.
Advance Context-Sensitive Management Theory
Treat local and African organisational realities as primary sources of management knowledge generation.
