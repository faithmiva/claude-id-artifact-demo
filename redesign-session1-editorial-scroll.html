Final Key Takeaways

Executive Summary

Philosophy as the Foundation of Executive Practice

Management thought is a disciplined lens for interrogating executive practice, organisational structures, and research design. African organisational contexts must not be treated as imperfect copies of Western models.

Practitioner Action Items

Strategic Directives for Scholar-Practitioners

  • Audit Organisational Control Systems: identify hidden assumptions about human motivation, authority and control.
  • Articulate Epistemic Stances: define evidentiary assumptions before approving major strategies.
  • Integrate Critical Realism: uncover hidden structural mechanisms behind failures.
  • Advance Context-Sensitive Theory: treat local realities as primary sources of knowledge.
