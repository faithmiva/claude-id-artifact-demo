Final Key Takeaways

Executive Summary

Structural Control Needs Human Agency

Classical frameworks establish essential mechanisms for efficiency and coordination, but unchecked they generate paralysis and attrition. Behavioural perspectives are vital correctives — human dignity, motivation and psychological safety directly dictate operational outcomes. High-performing organisations pair intelligent structural control with high psychological safety.

Calibrate Structural Controls to Context
