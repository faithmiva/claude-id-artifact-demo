Final Key Takeaways

Executive Summary

Management thought is a disciplined lens for interrogating executive practice and research design. Cultivate epistemic humility: empirical data is interpreted through philosophical filters, and African contexts are not imperfect copies of Western models.

Practitioner Action Items

  • Audit Organisational Control Systems: identify hidden assumptions in dashboards and KPIs.
  • Articulate Epistemic Stances: define evidentiary assumptions before approving strategy.
  • Integrate Critical Realism: uncover hidden structural mechanisms behind failures.
  • Advance Context-Sensitive Theory: treat local realities as primary knowledge sources.
