Executive Summary

Classical frameworks (Taylor, Fayol, Weber) establish essential mechanisms for efficiency, coordination and predictability, but unchecked they generate administrative paralysis, employee silence and workforce attrition. Behavioural and human relations perspectives (Mayo, Maslow, Herzberg, McGregor, Barnard, Follett) are vital correctives, showing that human dignity, motivation and psychological safety directly dictate operational outcomes. High-performing organisations pair intelligent structural control with high psychological safety and relational trust.

Practitioner Action Items

  • Calibrate Structural Controls to Context: Audit workflows, approval hierarchies and surveillance systems to distinguish controls that protect compliance from those that create delay or gaming.
  • Build Architecture for Employee Voice: Establish psychological safety protocols and participative channels that let frontline staff report failures without fear of retribution.
  • Integrate Informal Authority Networks: Engage informal influencers to build legitimate buy-in rather than relying solely on position power.
  • Engage Epistemic Humility in Executive Decisions: Interrogate performance data for the human assumptions and contextual realities beneath surface indicators.
  • Ground Doctoral Research in Dual Traditions: Bridge classical structural variables with human relations mediators in DBA research design.
